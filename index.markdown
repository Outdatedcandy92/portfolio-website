I am a senior at Leaside High School with a passion for electrical and computer engineering. I love designing PCBs, building hardware projects, and turning *~~stupid~~* ideas into reality

<br>

I’ve also been an active member of the *[Hack Club](https://hackclub.com/)* community, where I ran an event called *[Silicon](https://silicon.hackclub.dev/)*. In this event, teenagers designed and built their own development boards and had them fabricated. I mentored participants through the process, and many went on to create their very first devboard or even their first PCB. In the end, almost 20 teenagers manufactured their own boards, with $2,500 in funding awarded to support their projects. Seeing their work come to life has been one of the most rewarding parts of my journey.
